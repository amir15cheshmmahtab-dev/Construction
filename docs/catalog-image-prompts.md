# Phase 3 catalog product imagery

Generated on 2026-10-30 using the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool in default built-in mode. These are independent AI-generated illustrative product packshots for the local mock catalog, not photographs of confirmed commercial stock. No brand, manufacturer, certification, or product accuracy claim is implied. No CLI/API fallback was used and no generation failed.

Existing product images were inspected first. The five matching homepage assets remain unchanged and are reused from `/images/products/`: `cement.webp`, `brick.webp`, `rebar.webp`, `tiles.webp`, and `faucet.webp`, including their existing `-320.webp` variants. New assets cover the nine remaining requested product types. Each new packshot is a separate original generation, never a collage crop.

## Processing and verification

The original generated PNGs are retained in Codex's generated-images directory. Final RGB WebP assets are saved locally in `public/images/catalog/`, using a 640 × 640 primary and 320 × 320 responsive variant (Lanczos downscale, WebP quality 86, method 6). No homepage assets are replaced. Outputs were visually reviewed for subject, proportions, complete framing, neutral background, and absence of text, brands or watermarks.
